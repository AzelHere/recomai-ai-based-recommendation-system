# Attribution and data licenses

Application code and educational metadata have different licenses. Preserve per-resource authors, official URL, license and origin notes. This snapshot is for a noncommercial university project.

MIT records: Massachusetts Institute of Technology, MIT OpenCourseWare and the faculty actually named in each record. [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/) applies subject to [MIT terms and exceptions](https://ocw.mit.edu/pages/privacy-and-terms-of-use/). Descriptions were converted to plain text and primary categories derived locally. Only OCW records with license_cc=true were selected. No third-party images, videos, logos, enrollments or ratings are copied. Adapted MIT metadata must retain attribution, noncommercial use and share-alike conditions.

MedlinePlus records: Source: MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine. Only public-domain health-topic titles, summaries, groups and official URLs are included. [Content reuse policy](https://medlineplus.gov/about/using/usingcontent/), [official XML downloads](https://medlineplus.gov/xml.html). These permissions do not cover A.D.A.M. encyclopedia content, licensed drug/supplement material, stock images or restricted RSS content. Health resources are general education, not personalized treatment recommendations.

OULAD, offline research only: Kuzilek, J., Hlosta, M., & Zdrahal, Z. (2015). Open University Learning Analytics dataset [Dataset]. UCI Machine Learning Repository. [DOI 10.24432/C5KK69](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5KK69), [source and CC BY 4.0 license](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/349/open+university+learning+analytics+dataset). Associated publication: Scientific Data 4, 170171 (2017), [DOI 10.1038/sdata.2017.171](https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2017.171). Raw student files are excluded by project privacy choice; only aggregates, download instructions and the exact acquired checksum are included. Never deanonymize learners or treat their VLE IDs as Recom AI courses.


## Article metadata expansion

OpenStax reading sections retain per-book CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 as explicitly declared in the downloaded publisher metadata, with author/book/section/source attribution. Noncommercial/share-alike terms remain applicable. Microsoft Learn articles use the reviewed azure-dev-docs and azure-docs CC BY 4.0 licenses. NLM Genetics entries are government-authored Help Me Understand Genetics article metadata, not licensed encyclopedia content. No images or complete articles/chapters are redistributed. Every record in `processed/educational-articles.json` contains its exact attribution and metadata origins. See [the reviewed source report](../../docs/code-quality/source-and-license-report.md) and `manifests/article-acquisition.json`.
