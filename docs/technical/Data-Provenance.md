# Educational data and provenance
The earlier Phase 5 catalog had 1,865 resources: 200 preserved curated entries and 1,665 accepted genuine imports (1,465 MIT OpenCourseWare, 200 MedlinePlus). The validated acquisition snapshot has 1,770 records; 105 remain held, including 75 term variants and 30 ambiguous same-title editions. They were not merged merely because their titles match. Metadata normalization is not evidence that two courses have identical content.

Source IDs, external IDs, canonical URLs/hashes, source/license/attribution, metadata origin, source topics, import batch and acquired/check times are persisted. Exact retries preserve existing resources. Unknown provider difficulty remains UNKNOWN; duration and price remain null rather than fabricated. The 200 older curated entries have editorial estimates and lack the imported provenance guarantee. The original content remains on provider sites. The original MIT imports contribute about 61.9% of the expanded catalog, which limits provider diversity.

The Phase 4 bounded audit accounts for all 1,865 URLs: 323 accessible and 1,484 redirected/reachable; 45 policy exclusions, seven restricted and six redirect-policy results remain not fully verified. Zero confirmed missing after guarded repairs does not mean unrestricted reachability. The two conditional V17 URL fixes preserve IDs and owner relationships. URL checks are time-sensitive and are not a guarantee that future external pages remain available.

MIT OCW metadata is subject to its attribution, noncommercial and share-alike terms and third-party exceptions. MedlinePlus imports use only public-domain government health-topic summaries, not licensed encyclopedia/drug/image content. Per-record imported attributions accompany the submission. OULAD is a separate CC BY 4.0 research benchmark; its anonymous participants are not Recom AI accounts. Do not commercially republish the catalog without reviewing the actual rights. No global source-code license is invented: the owner must choose one before granting reuse permissions.

Acquisition and import steps are in Deployment-Guide.md and the original data/README.md. Fresh schema initialization does not require a private development dump. Imported metadata must be acquired/reviewed through the existing pipeline before it is added to a new deployment.


## Current article integration update

The current inspected catalog has 2,365 resources: 200 preserved curated entries and 2,165 accepted imports. The expansion adds 500 verified textual resources classified ARTICLE, including 399 explicitly labeled OpenStax textbook reading sections and 101 individual explanatory articles. The ARTICLE filter now returns 505 resources. Original 1,465 MIT OCW imports, 200 MedlinePlus guides and 105 held edition reviews are retained.

The Technology category previously contained zero ARTICLE rows, while the global Articles API already returned 15. Verified ingestion excluded ARTICLE. Explore also advertised unsupported BOOK, and Topic was folded into OR-based search instead of independently intersecting filters. The correction accepts reviewed ARTICLE imports, aligns actual enum options, adds a bounded topic predicate and provider input, supplies an accurate empty state and adds administrator type filtering. Read Article actions use the existing cards. No article-specific ranking boost or completion-on-click was added. Flyway V18 adds approved source registrations and guards ten existing landing-page type corrections; V1 through V17 are unchanged.

Historical evaluation scope: the reported catalog NDCG@5 values .678866 and .885226 were measured on the earlier 1,665 accepted imported resources within the 1,865-resource catalog, using seven queries and 210 assistant-assigned judgments. They were NOT measured on the expanded article catalog. The independent OULAD task and its cutoff/eligibility limits remain unchanged. Current regression checks verify ranking behavior and article inclusion; no expanded-catalog NDCG or learning-outcome improvement is claimed.

New metadata sources: Microsoft Learn Java fundamentals and SQL overview (CC BY 4.0), OpenStax book-specific CC BY 4.0 or CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, MedlinePlus Genetics (government text metadata). Short publisher excerpts and original links are retained with attribution; full chapters, images and third-party material are excluded. OpenStax reading sections are not standalone journal/news articles. Noncommercial/share-alike restrictions remain applicable. The application uses TF-IDF and deterministic ranking, not an LLM content ingestion or training service.
